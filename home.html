About SINTRO

SINTRO employs proprietary quantitative and systematic strategies for listed assets. We emphasize an opportunistic, algorithmic, risk-controlled and value-oriented approach to investing. Our primary objective is to deliver the most consistent returns, while minimizing risk.

Our Approach

We believe in a systematic and methodological approach to investing; without the interference of behavioral biases. Our strong culture of rigorous analysis and scientific research, supported by an algorithmic trading infrastructure, is the bedrock of our quantitative investment process. At the forefront of our innovation are Systematic-Traded Funds (STFs), a new investment sub-class designed to harness the power of algorithmic execution and data-driven insights.

Our Mindset

Together we achieve more. We embrace bold ideas and seize opportunities others don't, from the way we operate to the strategies we deploy. With a bias for action, our trades go into the market quickly. This rapid deployment is essential when addressing real-time challenges, particularly in the dynamic environment of financial markets. These markets offer instantaneous feedback, fueling our continuous cycle of learning and enhancing our impact.

Our Values

Family to family, entrepreneur to entrepreneur, investor to investor, we speak the same language. Deeply rooted in Europe, at the heart of Germany, we aim to promote our expertise throughout the world. Guided by trust, collaboration, and a relentless pursuit of excellence, we bridge generations and geographies to deliver impactful and innovative solutions that empower growth and consistency.
