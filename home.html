About SINTRO

SINTRO employs quantitative and systematic strategies for listed assets. We emphasize an opportunistic, algorithmic, risk-controlled and value-oriented approach to investing. Our primary objective is to provide the most consistent returns, while minimizing risk.

Our Values

Family to family, entrepreneur to entrepreneur, investor to investor, we speak the same language. Deeply rooted in Europe, at the hearth of Germany, we aim to promote our expertise throughout the world. We develop systematic strategies and tailor-made services, drawing on the depth of our know-how and the quality of our people. We serve our people with unwavering attention and commitment, building with them a close, trusting, and long-lasting relationship.

Our Mindset

Together we achieve more. We embrace bold ideas and seize opportunities others don't, from the way we operate to the strategies we deploy. These markets offer instantaneous feedback, fueling our continuous cycle of learning and enhancing our impact. It's our collective intellect, skill and ambition that allow us to achieve far more together than we ever could on our own. Whatever brings you here, this is a place where extraordinary and diverse minds come together.
